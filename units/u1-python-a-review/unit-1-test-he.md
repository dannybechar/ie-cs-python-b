- הגיע ל-100 או יותר ← `Goal reached after N deposits`

| הקלט | הפלט |
|---|---|
| 60, 50 | `Goal reached after 2 deposits` |
| 10, 10, 10, 10, 10 | `Goal not reached. Total: 50` |
